# UI brief

## Niche
Indonesian B2B engineering and manufacturing of waste incinerator machinery and thermal waste management systems (AtasiLimbah, atasilimbah.com).

## Audience
Facility managers, educational institutions (pesantren, boarding schools, campuses), commercial operators (hotels, markets, residential developments), factory EHS managers, and licensed waste contractors. They need to trust that AtasiLimbah is an authentic Indonesian equipment manufacturer with verified engineering parameters, real combustion capabilities, emission control options, and honest operational boundaries.

## Primary job
Provide transparent technical information on incinerator types (Dry, Wet, Specific B3, Custom) and enable qualified B2B buyers to evaluate capacity/specifications and request a formal quotation (RFQ).

## Primary action
Kirim Permintaan Penawaran (Submit Request for Quotation) or technical consultation on machine fit.

## References wanted
Industrial equipment manufacturers and thermal engineering leaders with clean specification matrices, real fabrication evidence, and structured inquiry workflows.

## Non-goals
E-commerce cart/checkout, consumer cleaning service aesthetic, AI slop badges/sparkles, aggressive floating chat bubbles, fake customer reviews, or unverified zero-emission marketing claims.
